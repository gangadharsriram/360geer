## Scale / dataset-conversion work
- Densification threshold 0.00006 is not scale invariant; RoMa sets are ~9-12× larger than the original (Sim3 fit of camera centres: all_q8 0.083, bricks_roma_openmvg 0.111). `scripts/rescale_openmvg_dataset.py SRC DST SCALE` writes a rescaled copy (source untouched), drops pcd points sitting exactly on camera centres (OpenMVG scene.ply draws cameras as glyph points) and links images from the views JSON `root_path`.
- COLMAP→OpenMVG conversion validated by reprojecting 77,003 keypoint observations (median 1.74 px).
- Scripts written: `run_bricks_roma_q8_fullres.sh`, `run_bricks_roma_all_q8_fullres.sh`, `run_bricks_roma_openmvg_fullres.sh`, `colmap_points_to_pcd.py`.
