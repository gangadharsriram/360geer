## Threads
- **Benchmark source**: `/data2/Resuts/360/pfgs360_outputs/Ricoh360/bricks/pfgs360/` (nerfstudio, COLMAP-free, UniK3D depth init and supervision, GNCC, joint pose optimisation, sky filter, 2.68M Gaussians, ~1M steps). Its points map into the original frame by a Sim3 (scale 1.085, residual 4e-16) → `scripts/pfgs_points_to_orig_frame.py`.
- **Per-view comparison** (`scripts/compare_with_pfgs360.py`, images in `output/compare_vs_pfgs360/`): PF-GS wins 47/50 views (median +0.91 dB); errors are in mid/lower latitudes; ours has more edge detail, not less.
- **Pose agreement reports**: Bricks 3-source report and all-12-scenes report (PDFs in `output/`). PF-GS-360 poses for all scenes were decoded from the checkpoints (`scripts/extract_pfgs_pose_adjustments.py`; ckpts were pickled with numpy 2 → alias `numpy._core`). 24/28 pairs Excellent/Good; the poses of the three sources agree to ~0.1-0.6 camera spacings and ~0.1-0.4°.
- **Schedule scripts**: `run_bricks_schedule_fullres.sh` (SCALE stretches all iteration settings), `run_bricks_control_and_long.sh`, `run_bricks_pfgs_init_fullres.sh`, `test_time_pose_search.py`.

## Bugs hit and fixed

- A mistaken earlier claim that over-densification (not VGG itself) caused the pixel-metric drop was disproved by the equal-count run (dense/thr 0.00015: 0.8327 / 24.68).
